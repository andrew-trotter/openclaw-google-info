# Privacy policy

Updated 30 October 2026.

## Google data

This personal OpenClaw installation accesses the owner's Google account through
Google OAuth. Access depends on the permissions granted during consent and the
tools enabled by the owner.

The installation uses email and calendar data to carry out the owner's personal
assistant tasks. This can include retrieving messages, preparing or sending
replies, organising messages, and managing calendar events.

## Processing and storage

The Google integration runs on the owner's computer. OAuth credentials and
tokens are stored on that computer, outside this public website. The `gog`
client stores its OAuth client secret and refresh token in macOS Keychain.

When the owner asks the AI assistant to process Google data, relevant content may
be sent to the configured AI model provider. Conversations, tool results, and
logs may retain that content according to the installation's configuration.
The owner controls the installation, its retention settings, and any backups.

This integration does not sell Google data or use it for advertising. This
website does not intentionally collect Google account data, set analytics
cookies, or provide a Google sign-in form. The hosting provider may keep normal
web request logs under its own privacy policy.

## Control and removal

The owner can revoke the integration in
[Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
Revocation stops future authorised access but does not delete previously stored
conversations, logs, or backups. The owner manages those copies separately.

This integration has no external users or customer support service. The owner
maintains the developer contact details shown in the Google consent configuration.
